User terms

Draft. Rizzora is a companion for conversation with virtual characters. Be kind. Do not use the product to harm a real person. A finished terms document has not been written.

Privacy

Draft. This preview keeps the demo sign-in, display name, chats, and sample balances in this browser. It does not send them to a server. A future service will need its own privacy policy. This page does not promise that a launched product stores nothing.

Membership and auto-renewal

Draft. The plan screen shows a reference offer: 50% on the first billing period, then the regular price, renewing until canceled. The 7-day trial is separate from the first three days of free text. What the trial includes, the renewal date, and the time zone are not defined here. Sample buttons do not create a subscription.

Virtual characters

Companions are fictional virtual characters, not real people. The product is meant to avoid pornographic or nude content, and conversation is meant to be moderated. This prototype does not run a moderation system, does not connect to a model, and does not review what you type.

No report form is included in this preview.
